Get your LinkedIn data export in 3 taps

  1. Open linkedin.com/mypreferences/d/download-my-data (sign in if asked).
  2. Pick "Want something in particular?" and check Profile — or just choose "Download larger data archive".
  3. Tap Request archive. LinkedIn emails you a link (usually within 10 minutes; sometimes up to 24 hours).
  4. Download the ZIP, come back here, tap Upload LinkedIn Data Export, and pick the file.

Your ZIP is parsed in your browser session — it's not stored on any server after the page closes.

Why not auto-scrape LinkedIn? LinkedIn's public API doesn't give third-party apps access to work history, education, or skills — only name, email, and photo. The Data Export is LinkedIn's own official channel for you to get your full data, and it works reliably. Any site that claims to "auto-import your whole LinkedIn" is either scraping (against LinkedIn's ToS and breaks constantly) or has enterprise partner access.
